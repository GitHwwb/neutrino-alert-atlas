Neutrinos through your area

Which IceCube astrophysical neutrinos traveled past you on their way to the South Pole detector? Each event below is a real high-energy neutrino with a reconstructed sky direction. We trace the straight line from source to detector and ask: how close did that line pass to you?

    How this works (and what it doesn't claim)

    Up-going event: marker at atmospheric entry point (neutrino traversed Earth to IceCube).
    Down-going event: marker at the sub-source point (source was overhead here at event time; no Earth traversal).

    • These are ~50 events per year, globally: IceCube's public Gold and Bronze alerts of likely-astrophysical TeV–PeV neutrinos. Not "neutrinos near you" in any per-location sense.
    • "Passed through your area" is a geometric reconstruction: the perpendicular distance from your location to the straight line between the inferred source direction and the detector. It's not a measurement at your location.
    • Direction errors (typically <1° for tracks, larger for cascades) make the trajectory a corridor, not a thin line. Distances under a few hundred km are inside the uncertainty for cascade-type events.
    • IceCube only sees TeV–PeV astrophysical neutrinos. The ~1014 solar and atmospheric neutrinos passing through every cm² of you per second are real but not in this dataset.

    Data: GCN AMON IceCube Gold/Bronze events. Updated periodically.
